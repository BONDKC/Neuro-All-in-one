Localize the lesion

Territory & brainstem localization

Posterior circulation — the 5 D's

  • Dizziness
  • Diplopia (double vision)
  • Dysarthria (slurred speech)
  • Dysphagia (trouble swallowing)
  • Dyspraxia (loss of coordination)

CSTAT — Cincinnati Stroke Triage Assessment Tool

  • 2 points: conjugate gaze deviation (≥1 on the NIHSS gaze item)
  • 1 point: cannot provide age or current month and cannot follow one of two commands (eye closure, hand open/close)
  • 1 point: cannot hold either arm up for 10 seconds before it falls to the bed

Disabling vs. non-disabling — ask the patient directly whether the deficit is disabling to them. This is a conversation, not a fixed cutoff.

05

🖥 Imaging

CT head (without contrast) — look for:

  • Hemorrhage
  • Eye deviation
  • Evolving hypodensity

CTA head & neck

  • Identify LVO/MVO.
  • RapidAI (software tool showing emergent CTH/CTA-H&N images) can help flag occlusions — but review the CTA yourself, don't rely on it alone.

CT perfusion — obtain per your attending's guidance on timing/candidacy.

Pearl Beware the "ghost infarct core" within the first ~3 hours from LKW — perfusion can overestimate core volume early on.

If unsure — discuss directly with the radiology resident in the CT reading room.

06

⚖ Reperfusion decision

TNK

Inclusion

  • Symptoms consistent with disabling ischemic stroke
  • Treatment can begin ≤4.5 hours from LKW
  • Age ≥18 years
  • Wake-up/unknown onset with DWI+/FLAIR− mismatch (if MRI available)

Absolute exclusions

  • Intracranial hemorrhage / SAH on CT
  • Extensive hypodensity (>⅓ MCA territory)
  • BP >185/110 despite treatment
  • Ischemic stroke <3 months
  • Prior intracranial hemorrhage
  • Severe head trauma <3 months
  • Active internal bleeding / suspected aortic dissection
  • Intracranial or spinal surgery <3 months
  • Infective endocarditis
  • GI bleed <21 days / GI malignancy
  • Blood glucose <50 mg/dL (reassess after correction)
  • Platelets <100K
  • INR >1.7
  • PT >15 sec / aPTT >40 sec
  • Full-dose LMWH <24 hrs
  • DOAC within 48 hrs (if normal renal function)

Mechanical thrombectomy — reasons NOT to proceed

  • Onset >24 hours
  • ASPECTS <6
  • No LVO identified on CTA/MRA/DSA
  • NIHSS ≤6 or non-disabling symptoms
  • mRS >1
  • Core volume >70 mL in 0–6 hour window is unfavorable
  • Core volume within 6–24 hours symptom onset (assess favorability)
  • <80 yrs old with core volume >51 mL and NIHSS ≥20 — unfavorable
  • <80 yrs old with core volume >31 mL and NIHSS ≥10 — unfavorable
  • >80 yrs old with core volume >21 mL — unfavorable
  • CTP mismatch <1.8 in MCA, M2, distal, or ACA — unfavorable
  • Posterior circulation LVO >48 hours or unfavorable imaging
  • Brain imaging not favorable due to hemorrhagic transformation
  • Family/patient refusal
  • Occluded artery unable to be accessed (anatomical cause)
  • Allergy to contrast material
  • Equipment-related delay
  • Delay in stroke diagnosis
  • No endovascular specialist available
  • Vascular imaging not performed
  • Advanced age

💉 After the decision

If TNK administered

  • No written consent required (per institutional workflow).
  • Discuss risks and benefits with the patient/family.
  • Admit under Stroke.
  • Level of care: Critical Care.
  • Stroke order set.

If mechanical thrombectomy

  • Keep bed slip under Stroke/Critical Care.
  • Notify NEA (neuroanesthesia).
  • Stroke order set.
