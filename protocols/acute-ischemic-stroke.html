Emergency

Acute Ischemic Stroke

Code Stroke workflow. Last known well (LKW) time drives every decision that follows — get it precisely, and get it before you do anything else.

Time-critical Confirm LKW time first
01

At the bedside

  • NIHSS immediately. ABCs, fingerstick glucose (hypoglycemia is a stroke mimic — treat and reassess before committing to the stroke pathway).
  • Confirm exact last known well time — call family/witnesses if needed. "Found down" patients get a wake-up-stroke workup, not an automatic exclusion.
  • Weight (for dosing), two IVs, stat non-contrast head CT + CTA head/neck.
02

IV thrombolysis window

  • 0–4.5h from LKW: check the exclusion checklist (recent surgery/bleed, anticoagulation status, BP, platelet count, glucose) and treat if eligible — don't wait on labs you don't need (e.g. INR if no history of anticoagulant use or liver disease).
  • Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg): 10% bolus over 1 min, remainder over 60 min — OR tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg (max 25 mg) single bolus, per institutional preference.
  • BP must be <185/110 before lytic and <180/105 for 24h after.
  • 4.5–24h (or unknown onset): consider based on perfusion imaging (CT or MRI perfusion mismatch) — this is an extended-window decision, loop in your attending/stroke team before proceeding.
Pearl Don't let a "soft" contraindication (mild symptoms, rapidly improving deficit) automatically take lytics off the table — a disabling deficit can still warrant treatment even if it's improving. This is a discussion, not an automatic exclusion.
03

Large vessel occlusion → thrombectomy

  • CTA showing LVO (ICA, M1, proximal M2, basilar) with a disabling deficit → activate the thrombectomy pathway/call IR-neurointervention immediately, in parallel with (not after) lytic administration.
  • Thrombectomy window extends out to 24h from LKW in appropriately selected patients with favorable perfusion imaging — LKW beyond 4.5h is not itself an exclusion for this pathway.
04

Post-treatment / not a candidate

  • Post-lytic: neuro checks + BP checks per your unit's frequency protocol, hold antiplatelets/anticoagulants for 24h, repeat CT at 24h before starting them.
  • Not a lytic/thrombectomy candidate: permissive hypertension (don't treat BP acutely unless very high or another indication) and start antiplatelet therapy per your unit's timing once hemorrhage is excluded.
  • Workup in parallel: telemetry, echo, lipid panel, HbA1c, carotid imaging — find the mechanism regardless of the acute treatment path taken.
Call your attending immediately Any potential LVO, any lytic-eligible patient, or any neurologic decline after treatment (think symptomatic hemorrhagic transformation — stop the infusion if still running, stat non-contrast CT, coags, consider cryoprecipitate/reversal per protocol).
